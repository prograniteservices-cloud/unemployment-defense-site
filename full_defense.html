Annotated Evidence List

This page details the physical and digital evidence supporting the claim of "Inability due to Employer Negligence" and "Retaliation."

EXHIBIT A: Overtime Ban Notice

Sept 18, 2025
Legal Relevance: Proves the employer removed the ability to finish work. As the last person in the line, receiving work late meant you *had* to leave it unfinished to comply with this rule.

EXHIBIT B: Request for Helper (Ignored)

Aug 13, 2025
Legal Relevance: Proves you proactively communicated the need for resources. The employer failed to provide them, creating the "inefficiency."

EXHIBIT C: Quality Risk Warning (Proactive)

Aug 6, 2025
Legal Relevance: Proves management knew quality was at risk due to scheduling issues *months* before firing you. It wasn't "negligence," it was a known systemic issue accepted by Robbie ("I gotcha").

EXHIBIT D: First Write-up (Retaliation Trigger)

Nov 13, 2025
Legal Relevance: This date coincides with when they learned you were moving. Prior to this, your attendance (within the 10-day allowance, used less than half) was not an issue. PROVES RETALIATION.

EXHIBIT E: Quality Write-up (No Tool Provided)

Dec 4, 2025
Legal Relevance: This write-up was for "Work Performance" due to lack of time, not negligence. An entire cart of tops (a full day's worth of work) was pushed to my area at 4:00 PM the previous day. I explicitly told Robbie and Shop Manager Andrew Bazil that I did not have enough time to finish. Robbie verbally said "that's fine," yet wrote me up the next day. Andrew Bazil can testify that completing this volume in the remaining hour was physically impossible.

EXHIBIT F: Incoming Workflow Condition

Daily Occurrence
Legal Relevance: Shows the "trashy" state of work arriving at your station. Without a helper or overtime, fixing this volume was physically impossible.

EXHIBIT G: After-Hours Work Left for Morning

Work done after 5 PM
Legal Relevance: Notice the timestamp. This work was done after 5 PM (after I clocked out) and left for me to find in the morning. This proves the machine work was consistently unfinished/poor quality ("trashy"), requiring me to fix it immediately upon arrival.

EXHIBIT H: Impossible Morning Deadlines

Before Overtime Ban
Legal Relevance: This shows an entire cart of islands left incomplete. Before the ban, I would come in early to fix this. After the ban, I was expected to manually hand-polish these defects (which should be machine-done) before 8 AM standard start time. This is physically impossible without overtime.

EXHIBIT I: Ignored Tape Measure Request

Text Message with Shop Manager
Legal Relevance: Proves I explicitly requested a tape measure from Andrew Bazil (Shop Manager) and was ignored. His response expressing frustration ("they don't care") proves that the lack of tools was a known management failure. Being fired for a measurement error after being refused a measuring tape is "Inability due to Employer Negligence."
