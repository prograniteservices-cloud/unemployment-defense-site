Opening Statement

Subject: Appeal of Unemployment Benefits Denial

Core Argument: No Misconduct. Discharge was due to Inability caused by Employer Negligence (Lack of Tools & Resources) and Pretextual Retaliation.

Introduction

I am appealing the denial of benefits because I did not commit misconduct. My discharge was for "Work Performance," specifically alleged "Inefficiency/Inability." Under South Carolina Regulation 47-101, discharge resulting from substandard performance due to inability, inefficiency, or good faith error does not disqualify a claimant from unemployment insurance benefits.

My Case: Set Up to Fail

I was a dedicated employee who worked 50–60+ hour weeks through June and July of 2025. What changed was not my work ethic—it was my working conditions. My failure to meet "performance standards" was a direct result of factors beyond my control:

  • 1. Refusal of Essential Tools: I requested a tape measure for two consecutive months. I was written up on Dec 4th for a measurement error, and only given a tape measure on Dec 5th—the day I was fired. Management's own response to my requests was: "they don't care."
  • 2. Tripled Workload & No Support: After my helper Nate was fired in August, my workload tripled. I was the only employee forced to hand-lift 800 sq ft of stone daily because I was forbidden from moving the 1-ton carts alone. On Aug 13th, I texted Robbie: "Do we have any plans to hire me a helper?" No help ever came.
  • 3. Sabotaged Quality Control: On Sept 18th, overtime was banned. As the last person in the line, I was receiving unfinished work late in the day. Overtime was my only tool to catch errors. I warned Robbie on Aug 6th: "We need adequate time to do quality control." He replied, "I gotcha," but nothing changed.

The Smoking Gun: Retaliation

I worked for months with zero write-ups. Then, on November 13th—the very day management asked about my plans to move—I received my first write-up. Three weeks later, I was fired. This was a pretextual termination to build a paper trail for cause.
