Opening Statement

Subject: Appeal of Unemployment Benefits Denial

Core Argument: No Misconduct. Discharge was due to Inability caused by Employer Negligence (Lack of Tools & Resources) and Pretextual Retaliation.

Introduction

I am appealing the denial of benefits because I did not commit misconduct. My discharge was for "Work Performance," specifically alleged "Inefficiency/Inability." Under South Carolina Regulation 47-101, discharge for substandard performance due to inability, inefficiency, or good faith error does not disqualify a claimant from benefits.

Core Argument

I did not willfully disregard my employer's interests. On the contrary, I was a dedicated employee who worked excessive hours to try and meet impossible demands. My failure to meet "performance standards" was directly caused by:

  1. Employer's Refusal to Provide Essential Tools: Specifically, a tape measure (refused for 2 months) and a crane (I was the only employee manually lifting 800 sq ft of stone daily).
  2. Unreasonable Workload: After my helper was fired and not replaced, my workload tripled, yet I was banned from working the overtime necessary to complete it.
  3. Retaliation: My performance was never an issue until I mentioned my intent to move (Nov 13). The disciplinary actions that followed were a pretext to terminate me for cause.

I ask that you find no misconduct and grant benefits.
