Closing Argument

Summary

The employer has the burden of proving misconduct. They have failed to do so. What the evidence actually shows is a dedicated employee who was set up to fail.

Key Points

  • 1. Impossibility: You cannot measure seams without a tape measure. You cannot manually lift 800 sq ft of stone daily (because using the shared crane required a helper which was denied) and do 15 jobs alone without fatigue. These are "good faith errors" caused by lack of tools, which is protected under Regulation 47-101.
  • 2. Pretext: The timing of the disciplinary actions—starting immediately after the "moving" conversation on November 13—proves that these write-ups were not about performance. They were about building a paper trail to fire a departing employee for cause.
  • 3. No Willful Neglect: I asked for help. I asked for tools. I worked late when allowed. I did everything in my power to succeed.

Request:

I respectfully request that the determination be reversed and that unemployment benefits be granted, as my discharge was due to factors beyond my control and clearly not misconduct.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
